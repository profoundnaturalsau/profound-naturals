Australian Native Botanicals

This product is derived from a plant native to Australia - grown and harvested on Country, shaped by the ancient soils, climate and ecology of this continent.

Australian native botanicals represent some of the most distinctive and biodiverse plant species on Earth. Many have been used by Indigenous Australians for thousands of years for their therapeutic, ceremonial and aromatic properties.

Profound Naturals is committed to sourcing Australian native ingredients ethically and sustainably - supporting local growers and honouring the deep botanical heritage of this land.
