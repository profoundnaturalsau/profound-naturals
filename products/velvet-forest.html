Botanical Perfume

Velvet Forest

50ml

Mediterranean cypress opens with a cool, green freshness before Australian native blue cypress deepens the accord with smoke and ancient earth. Baltic amber brings a warm, honeyed resin to the heart while creamy amyris sandalwood softens the base. Ambrette lingers as a velvety, pear-kissed musk - making Velvet Forest as close to living woodland as a bottle can hold.

Ingredients

Ethanol PGF4 (Perfumers Alcohol), Organic Baltic Amber (Pinus succinifera), Organic Cypress (Cupressus sempervirens), Organic Blue Cypress Oil (Callitris intratropica), Organic Sandalwood (Amyris balsamifera), Organic Ambrette Oil (Abelmoschus moschatus)
