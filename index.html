Profound Naturals Privacy Policy

Profound Naturals is a sole trader business based in Australia. This policy sets out how personal information is collected, used, stored, and disclosed in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. By using this website you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information Collected

Personal information is only collected when you actively provide it. Depending on how you interact with the site, this may include:

  • Purchases - name, email address, and delivery address
  • Gift cards - sender name, recipient name, and recipient email address
  • Newsletter subscriptions - email address
  • Back-in-stock notifications - email address and product of interest
  • Testimonials - name, email address, and review content

No customer accounts are created. No passwords are stored. Payment card details are entered directly into Stripe's secure environment and are never transmitted to or stored by Profound Naturals.

2. Purpose of Collection

Information is collected for the primary purpose for which it was provided, including:

  • Processing and fulfilling orders, including dispatch notifications and tracking
  • Delivering gift card codes to nominated recipients
  • Sending requested back-in-stock notifications
  • Sending the newsletter to subscribers (unsubscribe at any time by replying to any email)
  • Publishing approved testimonials on the website
  • Responding to customer enquiries

Personal information will not be used for any secondary purpose without consent, unless permitted by law.

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared for marketing purposes. It is disclosed only to the following service providers, strictly to the extent necessary to operate the site:

  • Stripe - payment processing. Stripe receives order value and billing details to process transactions. Stripe's privacy policy applies to data it holds: stripe.com/privacy
  • Australia Post - name and shipping address are shared solely for order delivery
  • Zoho Mail - transactional email delivery (order confirmations, notifications, gift card codes)
  • Netlify - website hosting. Netlify may log technical metadata including IP addresses and request timestamps for security and operational purposes
  • Google Fonts - typefaces are loaded from Google's servers. Google may log connection metadata. No personal data submitted on this site is shared with Google

No advertising networks, social media pixels, or analytics platforms are used on this site.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This site does not use cookies for tracking, advertising, or analytics. Anonymous, cookie-free page-view counting (GoatCounter) may be used to measure site traffic - it stores no cookies and collects no personal information. Stripe may set strictly functional cookies during the checkout process for fraud detection and session integrity.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are in place to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access. These include HTTPS encryption across the entire site, server-side handling of all sensitive operations, and no storage of payment data. No internet transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure; reasonable efforts are made to protect all information held.

6. Data Retention

Order records are retained for a minimum of five years to meet Australian taxation and business record-keeping obligations. Subscription and notification email addresses are held until removal is requested. Testimonials are held until a request for removal is received.

7. Access, Correction & Deletion

Under the Australian Privacy Principles, you have the right to request access to personal information held about you, to request correction of inaccurate information, and to request deletion where retention is no longer required by law. Contact hello@profoundnaturals.com.au to make a request. Requests will be acknowledged and actioned within a reasonable timeframe at no charge.

8. Complaints

If you believe your privacy has been mishandled, please contact hello@profoundnaturals.com.au. All concerns will be taken seriously and responded to promptly.

Returns & Refunds

How do I return a product or request a refund?
Email hello@profoundnaturals.com.au with your order number, the product name, a description of the issue, and a photo or video showing the fault. If the return is applicable under Australian Consumer Law, it will be processed promptly. Change-of-mind returns cannot be accepted. Refunds are issued for faulty products - for example, a defective spray head or damaged packaging on arrival.

Products

Are your essential oils 100% pure?
Absolutely. Every essential oil sold is 100% pure and undiluted - no carrier oils, no fillers, no synthetic additions. A simple way to verify purity at home is the paper droplet test - a quick internet search will show you how.

Are all products 100% natural?
Most are entirely natural. The exception is the alcohol-based botanical perfumes and colognes, which contain a very small amount (as low as 0.05%) of synthetic denaturant in the ethanol. This is a legal requirement under Australian regulations - ethanol sold outside of the food and beverage industry must include a denaturant to prevent oral consumption. Everything else in the formula is pure botanical.

Do you offer free samples?
Scent samples on paper strips are available free of charge - however shipping is not covered. Small 3ml testers are also available as part of smell tester kits sold on the website.

Workshops

How do I book a perfume or cologne making workshop?
Workshop booking details will be available through the dedicated booking tab on the website. Follow the prompts to select a date and secure your spot. If you have a specific enquiry in the meantime, email hello@profoundnaturals.com.au.

What do the workshops include?
A mix of theory and practical hands-on blending. Topics covered include the nature of scent blending, fragrance levels and note structures, scent strengths, chemical safety of natural components, and extraction methods used to produce pure oil concentrates. Participants leave with a real understanding of perfumery - not just a formula.

Are workshops suitable for children?
Workshops for children aged 8 and above can be organised. A parent or guardian must provide written consent and be present for the full duration of any workshop involving children.

Sustainability

Sustainability at Profound Naturals is an honest, ongoing commitment - not a marketing position. As a small business, the transition to fully sustainable packaging is a gradual one, and this page reflects exactly where things currently stand.

Packaging & Shipping

Wherever possible, excess packaging is eliminated entirely. Orders are shipped in padded mailer envelopes rather than boxes, reducing both material use and shipping weight.

Products are protected using recycled honeycomb wrap and recycled bubble wrap - still plastic, but sourced from recycled material rather than virgin. The goal is to move progressively toward fully biodegradable, paper-based packaging as the business grows. That transition is already underway.

Product Containers

All essential oils, absolutes, and perfumes are bottled in amber glass - recyclable, inert, and reusable. Amber glass also protects the integrity of natural oils from UV degradation, extending shelf life and reducing waste.

Lids are metal or glass depending on the product. Labels are not yet biodegradable - this is on the roadmap as sustainable label stock becomes more accessible at small-batch scale.

Aromatherapy Nasal Inhalers

The aromatherapy nasal inhalers are made from PET plastic - a durable, lightweight material chosen specifically because these inhalers are designed for long-term reuse. Refill wicks are available separately, meaning the inhaler body can be used and refreshed for years rather than discarded after a single use. Reusable and refillable is always preferable to single-use.

The Honest Position

Not everything is perfect yet - labels, some outer wrap, and certain closures are still conventional materials. That is acknowledged openly. Every product decision at Profound Naturals weighs environmental impact alongside quality, and that won't change as the range grows.

Baltic Amber

Baltic Amber essential oil is one of the world's most extraordinary aromatics - distilled not from living plant material, but from ancient fossilised tree sap. A gemstone in aromatic form.

What It Is

Unlike every other essential oil, Baltic Amber is steam-distilled directly from the amber gemstone - fossilised resin from ancient coniferous forests, millions of years in the making. The result is a warm, resinous, deeply complex oil with an earthy sweetness and a character that exists nowhere else in nature.

Ancient Civilisations

Ancient Greeks and Egyptians called it the "sun stone," prizing it for its golden light and therapeutic warmth. It was used in healing remedies for rheumatism, fever and digestive conditions, and burned as sacred incense in temples and burial rites.

Medieval Medicine

During the Middle Ages, amber was used to treat respiratory conditions including asthma. In times of plague, people burned amber resin and carried it as a talisman - believed to purify the air and protect against disease.

The Amber Road

From as early as 8000 BC, Baltic Amber was one of the most valuable traded commodities on Earth - carried along the famous Amber Road connecting the Baltic Sea to the Mediterranean, exchanged weight-for-weight with gold and precious metals.

Traditional Chinese Medicine

Known as Hu Po, Baltic Amber has been used in Chinese medicine for centuries to calm the mind, ease anxiety and support the nervous system. It is considered a grounding, settling material - anchoring restless energy and promoting clarity.

Why Natural?

The research on synthetic fragrance ingredients and human hormones is ongoing - but the concerns are real, well-documented, and have been known within the industry for decades.

Endocrine Disruptors

The primary concern with synthetic fragrances is endocrine disruption - chemicals that can mimic, block, or interfere with the body's hormonal system. This can happen in three ways: by binding to hormone receptors and triggering responses that shouldn't occur, by blocking natural hormones from functioning correctly, or by altering how hormones are produced, transported, metabolised, and eliminated.

The endocrine system governs reproduction, metabolism, growth, mood, and immunity. Disrupting it - even subtly, over time - is not a trivial concern.

The Chemical Classes of Concern

Phthalates are used as solvents and fixatives to make synthetic scents last longer. Some, particularly diethyl phthalate (DEP), are linked to reproductive health issues and potential hormonal disruption. While certain phthalates have been phased out in some markets, they remain in widespread use globally.

Synthetic musks such as galaxolide and tonalide have been detected in human tissue, breast milk, and the broader environment. Laboratory studies have shown potential for hormonal activity - and unlike most compounds, they bioaccumulate rather than being easily eliminated by the body.

Parabens, while primarily used as preservatives, have weak estrogen-mimicking properties and are commonly found in fragranced personal care products. Their cumulative effect across multiple daily-use products is an ongoing area of research.

Other synthetic compounds - synthetic fragrances can contain hundreds of individual chemicals. The potential hormonal and biological effects of many remain poorly understood, and long-term combination effects are rarely studied.

The Skin Absorption Factor

Fragrance applied to skin is not simply sitting on the surface. The skin absorbs compounds into the bloodstream - bypassing the digestive system and its filtration processes entirely. This makes the ingredient quality of anything applied topically more significant than it might appear for something "just worn on the skin." Wrists, neck, and chest - the most common application points - are areas with high blood flow and thin skin, increasing absorption further.

Industry Awareness - and Inaction

The fragrance industry has not been unaware of these concerns. The International Fragrance Association (IFRA) was established specifically to regulate ingredient safety - yet the same synthetic chemicals linked to endocrine disruption continue to appear in mainstream products at scale. The reason is straightforward: synthetic fragrance compounds are extraordinarily cheap to produce compared to natural alternatives, and the profit margins in mass-market perfumery depend on them.

Major fragrance houses supply ingredients to hundreds of brands simultaneously. The financial incentive to reformulate away from cheap, effective synthetics is low - particularly when ingredient disclosure laws allow everything to be listed simply as "fragrance," with no obligation to name individual compounds. Consumers are left without the information needed to make an informed choice.

It is also worth noting that many products marketed as "fragrance-free" still contain masking fragrances - synthetic compounds used to neutralise unpleasant chemical odours in the base formula. These are not required to be declared as fragrance ingredients. The problem is more pervasive than labels suggest.

Potential Health Impacts

Depending on the chemical, dose, duration, and individual sensitivity, endocrine-disrupting compounds have been associated with: reproductive health issues including menstrual irregularities and changes in sperm quality, thyroid dysfunction, metabolic disruption with links to obesity and insulin resistance, and neurological development concerns - particularly in offspring exposed during pregnancy.

Individual sensitivity varies significantly. Some people are more affected than others, and cumulative daily exposure across multiple fragranced products - shampoo, moisturiser, deodorant, cleaning products, candles - compounds the risk beyond any single product in isolation.

The Transparency Problem

In Australia, companies are not required to disclose individual ingredients under the term "fragrance." A single word on an ingredient list can legally represent dozens or hundreds of synthetic compounds. This makes it effectively impossible for consumers to know what they are being exposed to - or to make genuinely informed decisions about the products they use daily.

Why Natural Perfumes Are Different

Natural perfumes use essential oils, absolutes, and plant-derived isolates. These are complex by nature - plants produce hundreds of compounds - but they are naturally occurring substances the human body has had long evolutionary exposure to, rather than novel synthetic molecules it has not.

It is worth being honest: some natural essential oils contain compounds that have biological activity, and a small number have their own safety considerations at high concentrations. This is why IFRA guidelines exist, and why responsible natural perfumers - including Profound Naturals - formulate within them. The difference is that the risk profile of natural ingredients is far better understood, the ingredients are fully declarable, and the absence of phthalates, synthetic musks, and masking agents removes the primary classes of endocrine-disrupting concern entirely.

For those who want to know exactly what they are putting on their skin - and why - natural is the only category that makes that possible.
